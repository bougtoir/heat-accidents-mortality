## What remains incremental in the present study

1. **Seasonal-anomaly exposure definition**: locally unusual heat relative to each unit's day-of-year climatology, rather than absolute temperature or heat-wave indicators — separates "unusual for the season/place" heat from the seasonal driving-exposure envelope.
2. **Distributed-lag decomposition**: same-day excess vs 1–3 d deficit vs 4–10 d — quantifies short-term displacement (harvesting) explicitly; Wu 2018 used a same-period indicator, Basagaña 2015 daily counts, Pan 2024 DLNM on absolute temperature.
3. **Nationwide fatal-crash mortality** with contemporary FARS data (2016–2022) — extends Wu 2018 (2001–2011) by a decade and uses anomaly rather than heat-wave threshold.
4. **Road-user-specific fatal heterogeneity** at national scale (occupant/motorcyclist/pedestrian/cyclist plus age, hour-of-day, crash circumstance strata) — extends the vulnerable-user findings of Hsu 2025/2026 to US fatalities and adds circumstance checks.
5. **Attributable mortality burden** (deaths/year and fraction) computed from the fitted anomaly response — prior studies report relative risks, not an attributable count.
6. **External transportability assessment** to Japan using a harmonized anomaly design (rather than absolute temperature as in Pan 2024) — and the honest report that it does not clearly replicate.
7. Careful separation of association, attributable burden, and mechanism language.

## Novelty statements that must be removed/softened (done in revision)

- "under-recognised risk factor" (title) → new title without promotional wording.
- "fatal crashes have rarely been examined as a heat-related safety outcome" → false given Wu 2018, Pan 2024, Hsu 2025/2026.
- "little attention as a possible reservoir of unrecognised heat mortality" / "We are not aware of national population-level studies…" → replaced by explicit acknowledgement + precise incremental gap.
- "consistent with direct bodily heat exposure" → "compatible with differences in heat exposure, vehicle protection, travel behavior, and other mode-specific factors".
- "comparable to recorded direct-heat mortality" kept only with construct caveat.
- Warming projections reframed as illustrative scenario translations.
